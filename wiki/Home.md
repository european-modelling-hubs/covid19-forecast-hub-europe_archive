Welcome to the European Covid-19 Forecast Hub!

For forecast visualisations and reports, go to <https://covid19forecasthub.eu/>.

This Wiki contains technical details required for submitting forecasts to the European forecast hub.

In this Wiki, you will find guidance on how to [prepare for the first submission](preparing-to-submit), what [forecasts](targets-and-horizons) we are collating, how to structure your forecasts in the right [format](forecast-format), and how to submit via the github [web site](Submission-via-website) or [command line](Submission-via-command-line).

For those transitioning from the [US](Transitioning-from-the-US-COVID19-Forecast-Hub) or [German/Polish](Transitioning-from-the-German-Polish-COVID19-Forecast-Hub) forecast hub, there are specific sets of instructions.

This is an adapted version of materials provided by the [US COVID-19 Forecast Hub](https://github.com/reichlab/covid19-forecast-hub/blob/master/data-processed/README.md) and [Germany and Poland COVID-19 Forecast Hub](https://github.com/KITmetricslab/covid19-forecast-hub-de/wiki), both under the MIT license.
