We ask teams to submit a license together with their forecasts.


If you use a standard license, specify the short form of the license name in the `license` field of the meta data.

These are some standard licenses that are being used, with their metadata reference:

* [MIT License](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT): `mit`

* [Apache 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0): `apache-2.0`

* [Creative Commons 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): `cc-by-4.0`

* Other accepted licenses and their metadata are [here](https://github.com/reichlab/covid19-forecast-hub/blob/master/code/validation/accepted-licenses.csv)

We encourage teams to submit as a "cc-by-4.0" to allow the broadest possible uses including private vaccine production (which would be excluded by the "cc-by-nc-4.0" license).

If you use a separate license:
* Specify "other" in the `license` field of the [metadata](Metadata) file.
* Include a `LICENSE.txt` file in your `data-processed/team-model` folder
