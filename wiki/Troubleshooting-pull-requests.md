*The following is an adapted version of [materials](https://github.com/reichlab/covid19-forecast-hub/wiki/Troubleshooting-Pull-Requests) provided by the US Forecast Hub under the MIT license*

1. Create a Pull Request using the Github [command line](./Submission-via-command-line) or [website](./Submission-via-website).
2. Your PR should then appear [here](https://github.com/epiforecasts/covid19-forecast-hub-europe/pulls).
3. Several minutes after submission of your PR, you will be able to check if your PR passed or failed.
   * If your PR passed, your submission is complete.
   * If your PR failed, proceed to step 4
4. Click on "details":

   ![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/62434864/82876713-8023bf00-9f39-11ea-97c3-d097cf5175a3.png)

5. Click on "The build":

   ![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/62434864/82876868-b9f4c580-9f39-11ea-9fb3-dd03d20aee4b.png)

6. Navigate to the end of the log.

7. Check for "ERROR", debug, and try again

   ![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/62434864/82877468-823a4d80-9f3a-11ea-980e-6fe7e2d1fb6c.png)
